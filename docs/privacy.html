Privacy Policy

Kidney Guide (دليل الكلى) — last updated: 15 November 2026

1. Who we are

Kidney Guide is a non-commercial tool operated by one individual (the "Operator") who owns the TikTok account "دليل الكلى". The tool prepares educational videos about kidney health and publishes them, after the Operator's approval, to the Operator's own TikTok account.

2. Data we process

  • TikTok authorization data of the account owner only: when the Operator connects his own TikTok account, we receive an access token and basic profile information (account identifier and display name). These are used only to publish the Operator's own videos.
  • Content created by the Operator: videos, captions and hashtags.

We do not collect, store or process personal data about other TikTok users, viewers or visitors. We do not use cookies, advertising trackers or analytics on this website.

3. How data is used

Access tokens are used solely to upload and publish the Operator's approved videos to the Operator's TikTok account. Data is never sold, rented, shared with third parties or used for advertising or profiling.

4. Storage and security

Tokens are kept as encrypted secrets in the private settings of the Operator's code-hosting account and are not published. They are retained only while the connection is active.

5. Your choices and deletion

The Operator can revoke the connection at any time from TikTok (Settings → Security & permissions → Apps and services), which immediately ends access. Tokens will then be deleted. To request deletion of any data, contact us at the address below.

6. Children

This tool is not directed to children and does not collect data from children.

7. Medical disclaimer

The videos provide general educational information based on public-health sources and are not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest revision.
