Kidney Guide (دليل الكلى)

Kidney Guide is a small, non-commercial publishing tool that prepares short educational videos about kidney health for people living with kidney disease, in Modern Standard Arabic. Each video is based on information from trusted public-health sources (NIDDK, National Kidney Foundation, NHS, WHO) and is reviewed and approved by the account owner before it is published to the owner's own TikTok account.

The tool is used by one person only: the owner of the TikTok account "دليل الكلى". It does not collect, store or process personal data of other TikTok users.

The videos are general educational information and are not a substitute for medical advice from a doctor.

Connect your TikTok account (owner only)

The owner connects his own TikTok account once, so approved videos can be posted to it (Login Kit: user.info.basic; Content Posting API, Direct Post: video.publish).
